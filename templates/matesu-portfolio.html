About

Matesu is a design studio that believes in the power of structure. We build identities, editorial systems, and digital experiences rooted in typographic clarity and compositional discipline.

Every project begins with the grid — not as a limitation, but as a framework that liberates. From there, we choose type, space, and material with the same rigour a printer applies to paper and ink.

Founded in 2020. Based in Zürich. Working globally.
