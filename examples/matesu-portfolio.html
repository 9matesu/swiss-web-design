About

Matesu is a design studio rooted in the Swiss modernist tradition. We believe every project begins with a grid and a question: what needs to be said?

Our work spans branding, editorial, digital, and spatial design. We collaborate with cultural institutions, publishers, and brands who value clarity over decoration.

  position: absolute;
  inset: 0;
